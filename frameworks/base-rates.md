# Base rates

**Core question.** What's the reference class, and what usually happens to things in it?

**Apply.** Before reasoning from the specifics of this case, anchor on the outside view: how often do similar efforts succeed/fail, and by how much? Adjust from the base rate, don't ignore it.

**Surfaces.** Optimism bias, uniqueness illusions, and miscalibrated forecasts.

**Latticework.** Primarily `problem-2` (deciding under uncertainty) and `problem-4` (avoiding self-deception).
