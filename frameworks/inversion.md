# Inversion

**Core question.** What would guarantee failure here — and am I doing any of it?

**Apply.** Instead of asking how to succeed, enumerate the ways the plan reliably breaks, then design to avoid them. Restate goals as anti-goals.

**Surfaces.** Hidden failure modes, fragile dependencies, and "avoidable stupidity" that a forward-only view skips.

**Latticework.** Primarily `problem-2` (deciding under uncertainty) and `problem-5` (playing long games).
