# First principles

**Core question.** If I ignore convention and analogy, what is actually, necessarily true here — and what can I rebuild from those facts alone?

**Apply.** Strip the topic to its irreducible facts and constraints. Separate "what must be true" from "what is merely customary." Reconstruct the conclusion from the base facts.

**Surfaces.** Inherited assumptions, cargo-culted constraints, and cheaper/novel paths the analogy-driven view misses.

**Latticework.** Primarily `problem-1` (seeing reality), often `problem-4` (avoiding self-deception).
