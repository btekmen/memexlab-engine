# Second-order effects

**Core question.** And then what? What happens after the first, obvious consequence?

**Apply.** Trace effects two and three steps out: how do actors adapt, what incentives shift, what feedback loops form. Distinguish the immediate effect from the equilibrium.

**Surfaces.** Unintended consequences, induced demand, adaptation, and reversals that first-order thinking treats as static.

**Latticework.** Primarily `problem-5` (playing long games) and `problem-2` (deciding under uncertainty).
