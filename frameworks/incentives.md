# Incentives

**Core question.** Who benefits, who pays, and what is each actor actually measured and rewarded on?

**Apply.** Map the agents around the topic and the rewards driving them. Ask what behavior the current incentives produce — not what's intended.

**Surfaces.** Principal–agent gaps, gamed metrics, and conflicts of interest behind stated rationales.

**Latticework.** Primarily `problem-1` (seeing reality) and `problem-4` (avoiding self-deception).
