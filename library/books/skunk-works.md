# Skunk Works

Ben Rich's memoir of Lockheed's advanced-development division — how small, autonomous,
mission-focused teams (U-2, A-12, SR-71, F-117) repeatedly out-built large bureaucracies.

## Key ideas

- Talent density + autonomy beats headcount.
- Speed is strategy: rapid prototyping compounds into a structural advantage.
- Kelly Johnson's rules: small teams, empowered experts, minimal bureaucracy, fast iteration, prototypes, outcome focus.
- First-principles engineering over inherited precedent.
