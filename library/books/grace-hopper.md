# Grace Hopper and the Invention of the Information Age

Archival biography of Grace Hopper and the birth of software: the shift from machine-centric to
human-centric computing via compilers and high-level languages.

## Key ideas

- Computers should adapt to humans, not the reverse — the philosophical root of software.
- Abstraction creates scale: compilers unlocked whole industries on top of machine code.
- Software, not hardware, became the durable source of value.
- Standards beat fragmentation; innovation is institutional, not lone-genius.
