# The Art of Doing Science and Engineering

Hamming's Bell Labs / Stanford lectures on how great scientists think, learn, and choose
problems — an intellectual playbook for producing significant work.

## Key ideas

- Work on important problems; optimize for significance, not activity.
- "Luck favors the prepared mind": chance × preparation = discovery.
- Learning to learn is the meta-skill — build better mental models.
- Intellectual courage and independent thinking are strategic advantages.
- Knowledge compounds like capital.
