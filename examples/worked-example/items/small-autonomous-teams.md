# Small autonomous teams beat headcount

A small group of high-talent experts, given autonomy and shielded from bureaucracy, repeatedly
out-builds far larger organizations. Density and ownership, not headcount, produce breakthroughs.

## Cited facts

- Skunk Works delivered the U-2, SR-71, and F-117 with tiny, autonomous teams. Source: [[sources/skunk-works]].
