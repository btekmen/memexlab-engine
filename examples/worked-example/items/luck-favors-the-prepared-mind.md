# Luck favors the prepared mind

Accidents happen to everyone; prepared people recognize the opportunity in them. Discovery is
preparation multiplied by chance — so invest in preparation, the half you control.

## Cited facts

- Hamming reframed apparent luck as preparation meeting opportunity. Source: [[sources/the-art-of-doing-science-and-engineering]].
