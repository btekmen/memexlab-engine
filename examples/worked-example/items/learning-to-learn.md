# Learning to learn

Education is building better mental models, not accumulating facts. Independent thinking —
refusing to outsource judgment — guards against the self-deception of inherited consensus, and
is itself the meta-skill that compounds.

## Cited facts

- Hamming's subtitle, "Learning to Learn", treats thinking method as the durable advantage. Source: [[sources/the-art-of-doing-science-and-engineering]].
