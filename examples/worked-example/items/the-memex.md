# The memex

A personal device that stores a user's records and lets them build and follow **associative
trails** between them — a private, curated, mechanized extension of memory.

## Cited facts

- Bush names a hypothetical personal store-and-trail device the "memex". Source: [[sources/as-we-may-think]].
