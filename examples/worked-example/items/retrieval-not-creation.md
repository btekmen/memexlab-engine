# The bottleneck is retrieval, not creation

The hard problem of accumulated knowledge is **organizing and retrieving** it, not producing
more of it. The record outgrows any one mind's ability to traverse it.

## Cited facts

- Bush frames the growing scientific record as outpacing our means to consult it. Source: [[sources/as-we-may-think]].
