# Mechanizing the record

Compact storage (microfilm, photography in Bush's day) plus mechanized access makes a vast
record practical to hold and traverse — the substrate the memex needs.

## Cited facts

- Bush points to photographic/microfilm storage as the enabling substrate. Source: [[sources/as-we-may-think]].
