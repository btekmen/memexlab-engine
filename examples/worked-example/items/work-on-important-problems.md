# Work on important problems

Significance compounds; activity does not. Most people avoid hard, important problems because
they look impossible — which is exactly why working on them is leveraged. Optimize for the
quality of the question, not the volume of output.

## Cited facts

- Hamming repeatedly asked colleagues why they weren't working on the important problems of their field. Source: [[sources/the-art-of-doing-science-and-engineering]].
