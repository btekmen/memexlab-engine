# Associative trails

The mind connects ideas by **association**, not by rigid categories. A knowledge tool should
let a user lay down **trails** linking one record to the next, so a line of thought can be
re-walked and shared.

## Cited facts

- Bush argues associative indexing mirrors thought better than hierarchical classification. Source: [[sources/as-we-may-think]].
