# Abstraction creates scale

Every technological revolution rests on a new abstraction layer — writing over speech, money
over barter, compilers over machine code. Make complexity accessible and a whole industry
becomes possible on top of it.

## Cited facts

- Hopper championed compilers when many believed human-readable code was impossible. Source: [[sources/grace-hopper]].
