# Skunk Works

Ben Rich's memoir of Lockheed's advanced-development division (U-2, A-12, SR-71, F-117) — a
case study in how small, autonomous, mission-focused teams out-innovate large bureaucracies.

## Key ideas (paraphrased, for extraction)

- Talent density + autonomy beats headcount: a small elite team, protected from bureaucracy, ships breakthroughs.
- Speed is strategy: rapid prototyping and tight iteration loops compound into a structural advantage.
- Kelly Johnson's rules — keep teams small, empower experts, minimize bureaucracy, move fast, prototype, focus on outcomes.
- First-principles engineering over inherited precedent.
