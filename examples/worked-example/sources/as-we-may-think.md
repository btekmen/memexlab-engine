# As We May Think

Vannevar Bush's 1945 essay (The Atlantic, July 1945) introducing the **memex** — the
conceptual ancestor of this project. This is a worked-example **source note**: it records
provenance and the durable ideas extracted from the essay, not the essay's full text.

## Key ideas (paraphrased, for extraction)

- The bottleneck of knowledge is its organization and retrieval, not its creation.
- Hierarchical, categorical indexing fails to match how the mind connects ideas.
- The mind works by **association** — following trails from one record to the next.
- A personal device (the "memex") could store records and let a user build **associative trails** between them.
- Microfilm and photography make compact storage of vast records feasible.
- Mechanizing the scholarly record should prioritize intuitive, associative access over bureaucratic classification.
